📝 Exam Preparation

Sample questions for 2, 5 and 10 marks.

2-Mark Questions

1. What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence is a branch of computer science that develops systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.

2. Define Machine Learning.

Machine Learning is a branch of AI that allows computers to learn patterns from data and make predictions without being explicitly programmed for every task.

3. What is an Algorithm?

An algorithm is a finite sequence of clear steps used to solve a particular problem.

5-Mark Questions

1. Explain the applications of Artificial Intelligence.

AI is used in healthcare for medical image analysis, in education for personalized learning, in banking for fraud detection, in transportation for smart navigation and in agriculture for crop monitoring.

2. Explain the types of Machine Learning.

Supervised learning uses labelled data. Unsupervised learning finds patterns in unlabelled data. Reinforcement learning learns through rewards and penalties while interacting with an environment.

10-Mark Questions

1. Explain Artificial Intelligence, its types and applications.

Artificial Intelligence is a field of computer science concerned with building intelligent systems.

Types:
1. Narrow AI – designed for specific tasks.
2. General AI – a theoretical form of AI with broad human-like abilities.
3. Super AI – a hypothetical system that exceeds human intelligence.

Applications include healthcare, education, banking, robotics, agriculture, transportation and cybersecurity.

AI helps automate tasks, analyse large amounts of data and support decision-making.
